🌙 Moon Base Academy

Build a base on the Moon, dig up ice, make water and sell it to the rockets from Earth.
Start with the Welcome, then nine missions. Each one teaches something new. Finish one to unlock the next!

Welcome to the Moon! 🌙

You are here to dig up the Moon, find ice, turn it into water, and make your fortune!

Water is very important on the Moon. Everyone who comes after you will need it to drink, to grow food and to make rocket fuel. Moon Base Academy shows you how to get ice in this cold, dark and dusty place.

Tap each picture to find out what it does.

🎮 How to play: the game goes in turns. Each turn you make a plan: how much to dig, melt, sell and buy. Then press End turn ▶ and see what happens!
